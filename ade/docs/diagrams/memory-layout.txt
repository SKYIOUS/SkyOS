Desktop (heap, ~2KB struct)
  ├── WindowManager (Vec<AppWindow>)
  ├── DesktopIcons (Vec<DesktopIcon>)
  ├── ServiceManager
  │   ├── NotificationManager (Vec<Notification>, max 64)
  │   ├── ClipboardManager (16-entry history)
  │   ├── SessionManager
  │   └── PowerManager
  ├── IpcServer (Vec<Channel>, Vec<Message>)
  ├── ServiceRegistry (Vec<ServiceInfo>)
  ├── A11yTree (Vec<A11yNode>, 64-cap)
  ├── Profiler (fixed-size, no alloc)
  ├── Logger (fixed-size ring buffer)
  ├── CrashDiagnostics (fixed-size)
  └── Compositor (6 layer buffers, pre-allocated)
